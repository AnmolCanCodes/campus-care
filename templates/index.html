How it works

  1. 1
    Describe the issue

    Write it the way you would tell a friend. Pick a category and add your details.

  2. 2
    AI structures the complaint

    It writes a formal subject and email, and suggests a priority. It does not add facts you did not give.

  3. 3
    Review and send to the HOD

    Your email app opens with everything filled in. You read it and press send.

Your complaint is saved with your name and roll number so it can be traced. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
